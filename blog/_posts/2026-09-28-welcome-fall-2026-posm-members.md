## What we'll be exploring

There is a lot to explore across the lab in the coming months. Our recoil simulation team is working on extracting more precise power and energy estimates from our simulation models, investigating various relaxation modes of the materials, and developing a novel strategy to infer material properties from their recoil and loading behaviors.

Our mechanical redesign team is working on making improvements to the elastodynamic mechanical analyzer, with the goal of making recoil measurements more reliable and repeatable.

Our biomechanical modeling team is working on adding a large-angle model and sanity checks to the latch-mediated spring actuation (LaMSA) model. The team's ultimate goal is to model a genus of trap-jaw ants and understand how their morphology generates such rapid motion.

Our material synthesis team is working on creating materials with tunable viscoelastic properties.
